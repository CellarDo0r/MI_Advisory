About us

Specialist pharmacists dedicated to perinatal and paediatric care.

We founded Nurtured Medicine to ensure families and clinicians have access to clear, reliable medicines information when it matters most.

Our mission

To deliver compassionate, evidence-led medicines advice that supports the health of pregnant people, breastfeeding families, and children. We partner with healthcare teams to translate complex data into confident clinical decisions.

Our approach is grounded in real-world clinical experience, continuous professional development, and a commitment to patient-centred care.

What guides us

  • Respectful, culturally safe collaboration with care teams.
  • Clear communication for clinicians and families.
  • Evidence sourced from leading perinatal and paediatric guidelines.
  • Local partnerships with hospitals and community services.
