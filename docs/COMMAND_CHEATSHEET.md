# Command Cheat Sheet

Use these exact prompts when working with me so I run your business operating system consistently.

## Core Trigger Phrases
- `Run business ops review: daily`
- `Run business ops review: weekly`
- `Run business ops review: monthly`

## Daily Prompt (copy/paste)
`Run business ops review: daily. Today focus: [insert focus]. Add decisions, update actions, and set tomorrow's Top 3.`

## Weekly Prompt (copy/paste)
`Run business ops review: weekly. Review risks, compliance, finance, and archive completed actions. Then set next week's Top 3 priorities.`

## Monthly Prompt (copy/paste)
`Run business ops review: monthly. Re-check assumptions, timeline, dependencies, and resource gaps. Propose next-month priorities.`

## Optional Add-ons
- `Also prepare a founder summary for this week in plain language.`
- `Also identify blockers that need external help.`
- `Also list decisions due in the next 14 days.`
- `Also produce a ready-to-send email update draft.`

## Procedure To Follow Each Cycle

### Daily (10-15 min)
1. Open `docs/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (create if missing).
2. Update: work completed, decisions made, open questions, blockers, next actions.
3. Add any strategic decisions to `docs/registers/decision_log.md`.
4. Update `docs/registers/action_register.md` and set Next-Day Top 3.

### Weekly (30-45 min)
1. Review `docs/registers/risk_register.md` and refresh mitigations/review dates.
2. Review `docs/registers/compliance_tracker.md` and move deadlines forward.
3. Review `docs/registers/finance_tracker.md` and update estimated vs actual cost.
4. Move completed tasks from `action_register.md` to `archive_completed_actions.md`.
5. Confirm next week's Top 3 priorities.

### Monthly (60 min)
1. Review `docs/registers/assumptions_log.md` and validate or close assumptions.
2. Re-baseline timeline/milestones and dependencies.
3. Identify resourcing or advisor gaps.
4. Update priority roadmap for the next month.

## File Map (where everything lives)
- Dashboard: `docs/00_INDEX.md`
- Cadence guide: `docs/CADENCE.md`
- Daily logs: `docs/daily/`
- Registers: `docs/registers/`
- Templates: `docs/templates/`

## Fast Rule
If unsure what to ask, use:
`Run business ops review: daily and tell me the most important thing I should do next.`
